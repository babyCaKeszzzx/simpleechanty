02 — Instagram Algorithm & SEO

What the algorithm actually rewards

Six signals that directly impact reach, discoverability, and follower growth. Every post in this plan is designed around at least one of them.

01
Saves over Likes
Instagram weights saves and shares more than likes. Educational carousels (Pilates Dictionary, Muscles Targeted, Reformer Anatomy) are built to be saved — this signals high-value content and extends post lifespan.
02
Keyword SEO in Captions
Instagram indexes captions like a search engine. Every post should include: "pilates Quezon City," "pilates White Plains QC," "reformer pilates Manila." Embed these naturally in caption text — don't rely on hashtags alone.
03
Reels for Discovery
Reels are shown to non-followers — they are FolkStudio's primary growth engine. Hook within 1.5 seconds, use trending audio weekly, and keep captions keyword-rich. Strong reels compound reach over time.
04
Consistent Cadence
The algorithm rewards consistency over sporadic brilliance. 5–6 posts per week on a fixed schedule trains both the algorithm and the audience. Never go more than 2 days without a Story — Stories maintain daily presence.
05
First-Hour Engagement
The first 60 minutes after posting determines reach ceiling. Post at 7–9AM or 7–9PM for Manila-based audiences. Include a comment-prompting CTA in every post: "Which reformer move would you try first?"
06
Series = Return Visits
Recurring series like "Folk Talks," "Pilates Dictionary," and "Move with Folk" train followers to return regularly. Each return signals active follower interest — improving distribution of all future posts.

05 — Instagram SEO & Growth Tips

Specific actions for White Plains QC discoverability

Applied directly to FolkStudio's account and location — not generic advice. These are the exact moves that improve reach in the QC/Manila Filipino wellness IG market.

Caption Keywords to Use Every Post
Always include "pilates White Plains," "pilates Quezon City," or "pilates Manila" somewhere in the caption — not just hashtags. IG's search engine indexes caption text.
Use "reformer pilates Philippines," "beginner pilates QC," and "pilates studio White Plains" in at least 3 posts per week to build keyword authority.
Name the studio in every caption: "at FolkStudio, White Plains, Quezon City" — not just @folkstudiopilates. The text name is indexed; the handle alone is not.
Hashtag Strategy (Max 5, Targeted)
Use 3–5 hashtags only — not 30. Over-tagging signals spam in 2026. Choose: #pilatesPH #pilatesManila #reformerpilates #pilatesQC #folkstudio
Mix: one large (#pilates), one medium (#reformerpilates), one local (#pilatesQC or #pilatesWhitePlains), one branded (#folkstudioph).
Create branded hashtag for the challenge: #MoveWithFolk — every UGC post tagged extends reach to new audiences for free.
Reel Hooks That Beat the Algorithm
First 1.5 seconds of every reel must have text on screen + audio. Start with the payoff, not the setup — IG decides reach in the first 3 seconds based on watch time.
Use trending audio from the IG Reels audio tab — check every Monday. Adoption within 48 hours of a trend gets a distribution boost.
End every reel with a CTA on screen: "Book your mat → link in bio" or "Save for your next class." Saves and shares are the signals the algorithm rewards most.
Best Posting Times for PH Audience
Best windows for Metro Manila wellness audience: 7:00–8:30 AM (morning scroll before work) and 7:30–9:30 PM (after-dinner scroll). Confirm with FolkStudio's own analytics after 4 weeks.
Engage with comments within the first 60 minutes of posting — replying in the first hour signals active engagement to the algorithm, boosting distribution.
After posting, spend 10 minutes engaging with pilates/wellness/QC accounts. This "neighboring" signals account relevance to the algorithm and improves discovery.
